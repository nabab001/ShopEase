QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, payments, delivery, and returns on ShopEase.

How do I track my order status?

You can track your order at any time by clicking the Track Orders button in the top navigation bar. Every order is assigned a unique server receipt ID (e.g. SE-XXXXXX) and displays estimated delivery dates, live fulfillment status, and items breakdown.

What are the shipping charges and delivery timelines?

We provide FREE Standard Delivery on all orders of ₹2,500 and above. For orders under ₹2,500, a flat nominal delivery fee of ₹79 applies. Most orders are dispatched within 24 hours and delivered within 3 to 5 business days across major Indian cities.

Which payment methods are supported?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) across all deliverable postal codes, as well as instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and Net Banking with top Indian financial institutions.

How does the 10-Day Return & Exchange policy work?

If you're not fully satisfied with an item, you can initiate a return or exchange within 10 days of delivery. Our courier partner will pick up the package from your doorstep at zero extra cost, and your refund or exchange will be processed upon pickup verification.

How do I use promo code SHOP10?

Coupon SHOP10 gives you an extra 10% discount (up to ₹500 off) on all orders of ₹1,000 or more. Our backend automatically calculates and applies eligible savings directly in your cart summary and checkout screen.

Are the products guaranteed 100% authentic?

Absolutely. ShopEase has direct tie-ups with verified brand manufacturers and authorized national distributors. Every product comes with standard manufacturer warranty and passes through quality inspection checks before dispatch.
